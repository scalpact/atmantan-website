You do not need to see an aura to know when your energy is off. The seven chakras are linked to very ordinary parts of daily life: feeling safe, feeling creative, feeling confident, giving and receiving love, speaking up, trusting yourself and feeling connected. When one of them is blocked, it usually shows.

## The signs

**1. You always feel unsettled about money or home (Root).** Constant worry about security, feeling "ungrounded" or restless in your body.

**2. Nothing feels enjoyable (Sacral).** Low creativity, emotional numbness, or guilt around pleasure.

**3. You cannot make decisions (Solar plexus).** Low confidence, people-pleasing, digestion that reacts to stress.

**4. You keep people at a distance (Heart).** Difficulty forgiving, feeling lonely even with people around you, or giving too much and receiving too little.

**5. You hold back what you want to say (Throat).** Fear of being judged, a tight throat when you are upset, or talking over others to be heard.

**6. Your mind will not switch off (Third eye).** Overthinking, confusion, ignoring your gut feeling and regretting it later.

**7. You feel disconnected or without purpose (Crown).** Feeling flat, tired for no clear reason, or cut off from your spiritual side.

## Simple things you can do at home

- Spend ten minutes outside barefoot on grass or soil to feel grounded.
- Breathe slowly into the area of the chakra that feels weak, for a few minutes each day.
- Use an affirmation for that chakra, such as "I am safe" for the root or "I speak my truth with kindness" for the throat.
- Keep a crystal for that chakra with you, like red jasper for the root or amethyst for the crown.
