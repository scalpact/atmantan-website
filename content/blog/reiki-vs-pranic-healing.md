I am trained in both Reiki (up to Grandmaster level) and pranic healing, and one of the questions I hear most is which one someone should choose. The honest answer is that both are gentle and safe, and the best choice depends on what you need.

## How Reiki works

Reiki is a Japanese healing system. The healer acts as a channel for universal life-force energy, and that energy flows to wherever the body and mind need it most. In a session the healer's hands rest lightly on or just above the body. Most people feel warmth, deep calm and sometimes an emotional release.

Reiki is a good choice when you:

- feel tired, stressed or emotionally heavy
- are going through grief, a breakup or a big life change
- want deep relaxation and better sleep
- are recovering from illness and want gentle support alongside your treatment

## How pranic healing works

Pranic healing was developed by Grand Master Choa Kok Sui. It is a no-touch method. The healer first scans the aura to find areas where energy is congested or depleted, then cleans out the used energy and fills those areas with fresh prana. Because it is so structured, it can be used for very specific concerns.

Pranic healing is a good choice when you:

- have a specific physical or emotional issue you want to focus on
- prefer a healer who does not touch you at all
- want to work on patterns like anger, anxiety or addiction (pranic psychotherapy)

## Can you take both?

Yes. Many of my clients start with a pranic healing session to clear a specific block, then use Reiki for ongoing balance and calm. Both can be done in person at the Navsari centre or from a distance.

## A note on health

Energy healing supports your body's natural ability to heal. It does not replace a doctor, and you should never stop medicine without your doctor's advice. I work alongside medical care, not against it.
