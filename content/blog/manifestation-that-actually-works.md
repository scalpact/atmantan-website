Manifestation has become a popular word on social media, and a lot of what you see makes it sound like magic: write it down, think positive, and it arrives. In my experience, what works is simpler and more honest than that. You get clear on what you want, you clear the beliefs that block it, and then you act in line with it every day.

## Step 1: Be specific

"I want more money" is too vague. "I want to earn enough to move into a two-bedroom flat by next June" gives your mind and your energy something clear to work towards. Write your intention in the present tense, as if it is already happening.

## Step 2: Find the belief that is in the way

Read your intention out loud and notice the first doubt that comes up. It might be "people like me don't earn that much" or "I always lose what I get". That doubt is the real work. Write it down, then write a kinder, truer sentence to replace it.

## Step 3: Use affirmations and mantras daily

Repeat your new belief morning and night. Short, simple lines work best. Many of my clients tell me that saying their affirmations consistently, even when they did not feel like it, is what shifted things for them.

## Step 4: Take one aligned action every day

The universe meets you halfway. If you want a new job, update one line of your CV. If you want a loving relationship, do one kind thing for yourself. Small actions tell your mind you mean it.

## Step 5: Let go of how it must happen

Hold the outcome lightly. Sometimes what arrives is better than what you asked for, just in a different form.
