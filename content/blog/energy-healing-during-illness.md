Some of the most meaningful work I do is with people living with long-term illness, and with families supporting someone through cancer treatment. I want to be clear and honest about what energy healing offers in these situations.

## What it can help with

Reiki and pranic healing work on your energy body and nervous system. During a difficult illness, that can mean:

- feeling calmer before scans, appointments and treatment days
- better sleep and less restlessness
- easing the emotional weight of fear, anger and grief
- feeling more at peace in your body
- support for caregivers, who often carry as much stress as the patient

Many clients tell me that the sessions give them a space where they can finally rest and feel looked after.

## What it cannot do

Energy healing is not a cure, and it is not a replacement for medical treatment. I will never ask anyone to stop their medicine, skip chemotherapy or avoid their doctor. The healing I offer is complementary. It works alongside the care your medical team gives you.

## How sessions work

For people who cannot travel, I offer distance healing. You simply rest at home, in bed or in a chair, at an agreed time. Family members can also learn simple Reiki self-healing so they can support their loved one every day.

## Talking to your doctor

It is always a good idea to let your doctor know you are using complementary therapies. Reiki and pranic healing are gentle and non-invasive, and most medical teams are comfortable with them.

If you or someone you love is going through a long illness and could use some calm and support, message me. We can talk first, with no obligation.
