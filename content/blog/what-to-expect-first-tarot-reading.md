Most people who message me for their first reading start with the same line: "I have never done this before." That is completely fine. A tarot reading is a conversation, not a test, and you do not need to know anything about the cards.

## Before the session

When you book, I ask for your first name and the area of life you want to look at, such as career, a relationship, family, money or a decision you are stuck on. You do not have to share every detail. A clear question gives a clearer reading, so take a few minutes to write down what is really on your mind.

Good questions look like this:

- What do I need to understand about my situation at work right now?
- What is blocking me from moving forward in this relationship?
- What should I keep in mind while I decide between these two options?

Questions that only ask "yes or no" can be answered, but you will get much more from a reading if you ask what, why or how.

## During the reading

Sessions happen on a phone call, video call or chat, whichever you are comfortable with. I shuffle and lay out the cards for your question, then explain what each card is showing and how the cards connect. You can stop me and ask questions at any point.

Tarot does not decide your future for you. It shows the energy around a situation, the patterns you may be repeating, and the choices that are open to you. The decision always stays with you.

## After the reading

Many clients like to note down the main message and the cards that came up. If something in the reading points to an energy block, I may suggest a simple affirmation, a short Reiki or pranic healing session, or a crystal that suits you. There is never any pressure to take more sessions.

## Is tarot right for you?

If you are looking for a fresh, honest perspective on something that feels confusing, a reading can help you see it more clearly. If you are dealing with a medical, legal or financial problem, please also speak to the right professional. Tarot works best alongside practical help, not in place of it.
