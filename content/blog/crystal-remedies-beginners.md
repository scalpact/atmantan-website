Crystals are one of the easiest ways to keep healing energy with you between sessions. You do not need a big collection. One or two stones, chosen for what you need right now and used with intention, will do far more than a shelf full of crystals you never touch.

## Choose by what you need

- **Amethyst:** calm, sleep, a busy mind. Keep it by your bed.
- **Rose quartz:** self-love, relationships, healing after hurt. Wear it or keep it near your heart.
- **Clear quartz:** clarity and focus. It also amplifies other crystals.
- **Black tourmaline:** protection from negative energy. Keep it near your front door or work desk.
- **Citrine:** confidence, money and new beginnings. Keep it in your wallet or cash drawer.

If you are drawn to a stone and cannot explain why, trust that. It is often exactly what you need.

## Cleanse it before use

Crystals pick up energy from everyone who handles them. Before you start, cleanse yours by holding it under running water for a minute (avoid this for soft stones like selenite), leaving it in moonlight overnight, or passing it through incense smoke.

## Set an intention

Hold the crystal in both hands, close your eyes, and say what you want it to help you with. Keep it simple: "Help me stay calm at work" or "Protect my home."

## Use it daily

Carry it, wear it, or place it where you spend the most time. Hold it for a minute when you need to reset. Cleanse it again every few weeks or whenever it feels heavy.

## Personal crystal remedies

In my sessions I often suggest a crystal remedy based on what comes up in your reading or healing. That way the stone matches your energy and your situation, not just a general list.
