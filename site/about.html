About Sheetal

Certified tarot professional, Reiki Grandmaster, pranic healer and manifestation coach.

My work

I have spent more than ten years in the healing practice. It began with pranic healing in 2014, and over time grew to include tarot, Reiki up to Grandmaster level, crystal healing and manifestation coaching.

My work is centred on helping you find clarity, shift your energy and align with your highest self. Whether you are moving through change, looking for insight or ready to manifest something new, I offer a safe, supportive space for real change.

I blend intuitive guidance with grounded practice. In every session you get an honest answer, explained simply, and practical steps you can take afterwards: an affirmation, a mantra, a crystal or a short daily practice.

Today I see clients online from anywhere, and in person at the Atmantan Reiki Healing & Meditation Centre in Navsari.
