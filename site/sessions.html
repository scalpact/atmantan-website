FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to come to the centre?

No. Tarot readings and manifestation coaching happen on phone, chat or video. Reiki and pranic healing can be done at the Navsari centre or as distance healing wherever you are.

How do I book?

Send a message on Instagram or WhatsApp with the session you want. The first consultation is free. We will fix a time that suits you.

Is distance healing really effective?

Energy is not limited by distance. In a distance session you rest at home at the agreed time while I send the healing. Most clients feel it the same way they feel an in-person session.

Will you tell me my future is fixed?

No. Tarot shows the energy around a situation and the choices open to you. Your decisions and actions shape what happens next.

Can healing replace my medicine?

No. Reiki and pranic healing are complementary. Please continue any treatment your doctor has given you.

Is what I share kept private?

Yes. Everything you share in a session stays between us.

Not sure where to start?

Send me a message and tell me a little about what is going on. Your first consultation is free, and I will tell you honestly which session, if any, will help.
